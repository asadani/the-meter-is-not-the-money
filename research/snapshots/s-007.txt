Stripe Acquires OpenRouter for $7B+, Turning Model Routing Into a Payments Infrastructure Problem

Stripe has finalized an agreement to acquire OpenRouter for more than $7 billion, according to a Bloomberg report published August 16, 2026. The acquisition, which follows initial reports of talks by the Wall Street Journal in July, marks a significant consolidation in the artificial intelligence infrastructure market. While a Stripe spokesperson told TechCrunch that the company does not comment on rumors or speculation, the deal signals a major shift in how financial services providers are positioning themselves within the AI ecosystem.

The $7 billion price tag represents a substantial premium, reflecting a 5.4x markup over the $1.3 billion valuation OpenRouter achieved during its Series B funding round just three months prior in May 2026. That round, which raised $113 million, included participation from high-profile investors such as Sequoia, Andreessen Horowitz, Menlo Ventures, and Alphabet’s Capital G. The rapid appreciation in value underscores the perceived criticality of the infrastructure layer that OpenRouter occupies.
